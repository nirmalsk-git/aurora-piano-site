Privacy Policy — Aurora Piano

Last updated: 8 September 2026

The short version. Aurora Piano runs on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your practice history, settings and recordings stay in the app's storage on your phone or tablet, and are not uploaded anywhere.

The only time the app talks to another company is when you buy a subscription — that purchase is handled by Google Play, and its status is managed for us by RevenueCat. Details are below.

Who we are

Aurora Piano is published by SNK App Developer, an independent developer based in Gujarat, India.

For any question about this policy or about your data, write to aurorapiano.support@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

The following is created by you as you use the app, and is written to the app's private storage area on your device:

  • Your practice and lesson progress, and streak information.
  • App settings — sound, instrument, keyboard, appearance and accessibility choices.
  • Recordings you make in the Studio ("takes"), and any files you import.
  • Latency and microphone calibration measurements.
  • Your consent choices (see Practice reminders below).

None of this is transmitted to us. We have no server that receives it, and no way to read it. It exists only on your device and in any backup you yourself make.

The microphone

If you allow it, Aurora Piano can listen to an acoustic piano so it can tell which notes you played.

Microphone audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. The audio is analysed on your device, as it arrives, purely to work out note pitches and timings. Only those detected notes are kept; the sound itself is discarded immediately and is never written to a file.

The microphone is used only while a screen that needs it is open, and only after you have granted Android's recording permission. You can revoke that permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app continues to work without it.

Recordings you save in the Studio are not microphone captures. They are re-created from the notes you played, using the app's own built-in instrument sounds.

Practice reminders

Practice reminders are off unless you turn them on. When enabled, they are ordinary local notifications scheduled by your device at the time you choose. No reminder involves a server, and no reminder data leaves your device.

Purchases and subscriptions

Aurora Piano offers optional paid plans. If you choose to buy one:

  • Google Play processes the payment. We never see or receive your card details, billing address, or payment credentials. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat manages the subscription status on our behalf — it is what tells the app whether your plan is currently active. To do that it receives a purchase receipt from Google, an anonymous identifier for your installation, and basic technical information such as your device platform, app version, and country. It does not receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

You can subscribe, and use every paid feature, without ever creating an account with us or telling us who you are.

Children's privacy

Aurora Piano is designed to be safe for children learning the piano, and we have built it so that using it does not require giving up any personal information.

  • The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
  • There is no advertising, no behavioural profiling, and no social contact between users.
  • A parental confirmation step stands in front of any purchase, so a child cannot buy a subscription on their own.

This posture is intended to meet the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) regarding children's data. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question or concern, please write to us at the address above.

Your control over your data

  • Erase everything. The app provides an option to delete all of the data it has stored, from within the app itself.
  • Uninstalling removes it all. Because everything lives in the app's private storage, removing the app removes your data with it.
  • Purchases are separate. Subscriptions are managed in your Google Play account, and can be cancelled there at any time.

Since we hold no personal information about you on any server, there is nothing for us to look up, export, correct, or delete on request — but if you believe otherwise, write to us and we will help.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area, which Android protects from other applications. Because we operate no account system and no server holding your information, there is no central store of user data that could be breached.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a future feature needs to send something to a server — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that feature ships. We will not quietly broaden what the app collects.
