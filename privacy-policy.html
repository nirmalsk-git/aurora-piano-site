Aurora Piano — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-28. Published by SNK App Developer, an individual developer in India. Questions: aurorapiano.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Aurora Piano has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Everything it knows about you lives on your device.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you send it: a feedback message, a recording you share, a backup file you save somewhere.
  • Every switch that would record anything starts OFF. The app never asks for a birthday, a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • Children under 13 get a kids set-up with less in it, and a grown-up check in front of every switch.
  • Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your card.
  • Erase everything on this device (Settings › Privacy) deletes all of it.

Who we are

Aurora Piano (the app) is published on Google Play by SNK App Developer, an individual developer based in India. The app's package name is com.pianoapp.aurorapiano. This policy covers the Android app; there is no website account, newsletter or online service behind it.

What the app keeps on your device

The app stores what it needs to teach you, in its own private folder on your phone or tablet, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Your set-up: a nickname you type (any word you like) and an age group (under 13, teen, adult). No date of birth is asked for or stored.
  • Your progress: which lessons, levels and songs you have played, your results, your practice plan, your streak and your review cards.
  • Your takes: the notes you played during a graded attempt, and any recording you deliberately make in the Studio.
  • Your imports: songs you bring in as MIDI or MusicXML files. They stay on the device and are never uploaded.
  • Your settings: theme, sound, Buddy voice, reminder time, keyboard and microphone calibration for this device.
  • Your subscription status: a small signed record of whether Premium is active on this device, so the app works offline. It holds no payment details.
  • An optional local log (see the next section), only if you switch it on.
  • A last-crash note, only if the app crashes, and only until you send it or erase it.

The app's data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. If you want a copy, Settings › Backup writes one file and lets you choose where it goes; nothing is sent anywhere by the app itself.

The optional improvement log

Settings › Privacy has a switch, Help improve Aurora Piano. It is OFF until you turn it on. While it is on, the app keeps a rolling 7-day log on the device of at most 2,000 short entries, each one of these events: screen_opened, take_completed, mic_rung, purchase_outcome, crash. Every entry is a fixed choice from a list or a small number (which screen, which level, which result band, which kind of error). The log cannot hold free text, a name, a recording, a file name, a song title, or any identifier for you or your device beyond "phone or tablet" and the Android version number.

The log never leaves the device on its own. The only way it goes anywhere is the Send feedback screen, where you can attach it to a message, read exactly what it contains first, and send it through an app of your choosing to aurorapiano.support@gmail.com. Turning the switch off stops the log; Erase everything deletes it.

For a profile in the under-13 age group the switch does nothing: no entry is ever written, whatever the switch shows, and the grown-up check does not change that.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — one gentle practice reminder a day, only if you turn reminders on in Settings, at the time you choose.
  • Start after a restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — so a reminder you set survives the phone being switched off and on. It does nothing else.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only while you practise with the microphone as your instrument, and only after the app has explained why and Android has asked you. The sound is turned into notes on the device and is not kept; nothing is recorded to a file unless you press Record in the Studio, and that recording stays on your device.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, photos, calendar, or access to other apps' files. Importing a song opens Android's own file picker; the app sees only the file you choose.

Children

Aurora Piano is for learners of every age. A grown-up sets the app up and says who is learning, and the app treats a child's profile differently by design:

  • Choosing the under-13 age group switches on the kids set-up: no social features, no messaging, no links out, and the improvement log permanently off.
  • Every consent switch, and the way to a purchase, sits behind a grown-up check — a short arithmetic question written in words. The app never asks for a birthday, and nothing typed into the check is kept.
  • The app collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. The nickname is a word the learner chooses and it stays on the device.
  • Where the law treats anyone under 18 as a child, as it does in India, a parent or guardian sets the app up and holds the grown-up check.

Parents and guardians can see everything the app holds by opening it, and remove it all with Erase everything on this device or by uninstalling the app. Write to aurorapiano.support@gmail.com with any question.

Purchases

Aurora Piano is free to use, and stays free. Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, the renewal, the cancellation and the refund, under Google's own terms and privacy policy; the app receives from Google only whether the subscription is active and until when. We never receive your card or bank details.

To keep your subscription working across reinstalls and offline, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, from the version that ships in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase and its status together with an anonymous, random app-user identifier that it generates and stores on the device — not your name, email or Google account. That identifier lets you restore a purchase; you can ask us at aurorapiano.support@gmail.com to delete it on RevenueCat's side.

How long we keep things

Everything above stays on your device until you erase it or uninstall the app. We keep nothing on any server, because there is none. A feedback message you send to us is kept in our mailbox for as long as it takes to act on it, and deleted when you ask.

Your choices and rights

  • See it: every screen of the app shows you what it holds — progress, takes, imports, settings — and the Send feedback screen shows the exact contents of anything you are about to send.
  • Switch it off: every consent switch is in Settings › Privacy.
  • Take it with you: Settings › Backup writes one file containing your progress and settings.
  • Delete it: Erase everything on this device removes every file and folder the app wrote (22 of them), or uninstall the app.
  • Ask us: aurorapiano.support@gmail.com. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 gives you rights of access, correction and erasure; because the app keeps nothing about you off your device, the controls above are how those rights are exercised. Learners elsewhere have the same controls.

Security

The app's files live in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. The app is built with Android's automatic backup switched off, ships without debugging enabled, and verifies its bundled content when it starts. Because nothing is sent to a server, there is no account to be breached.
