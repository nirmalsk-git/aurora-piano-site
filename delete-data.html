Delete your Aurora Piano data

Aurora Piano is published on Google Play by SNK App Developer.

Aurora Piano has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The only data we hold about you is a record of subscription purchases, linked to a random anonymous ID, kept by our subscription provider RevenueCat.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from any address:
    aurorapiano.support@gmail.com
    Subject: Delete my data – Aurora Piano.
  2. Include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). Find it in Google’s purchase receipt email, or in the Play Store app under Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history. Because there are no accounts, this is the only way we can find your record.
  3. We delete your record and confirm by reply within 30 days.

If you never bought a subscription, we hold no data about you. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

What is deleted and what is kept

DeletedYour anonymous app ID and purchase history stored with RevenueCat.
Kept by usTransaction totals in our accounting records, only for as long as Indian tax law requires. These contain no name or contact details.
Kept by GoogleYour Google Play order and payment records, handled under Google’s own privacy policy.
On your phoneSettings and progress stay until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings › Apps › Aurora Piano › Storage › Clear data).
Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel first in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. If you tap “Restore purchases” later, a new anonymous record is created.
